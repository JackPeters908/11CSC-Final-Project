From a Catholic perspective, every person has value simply because they exist. In today’s online world, it is easy to forget that behind every screen is a real human being, not just a profile picture or a target for mean comments. Having a social duty means carrying kindness into digital spaces by standing up to cyberbullying, avoiding online drama, and sharing things that actually help others. Ultimately, technology should be used to bring people together, look out for the vulnerable, and build up our real-world communities.
